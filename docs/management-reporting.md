# Management reporting and data coverage

Choose a company, reporting month and as-of date before a management review. Check Data Quality for completeness and reconciliation, then apply department, location and employment type where the dataset supports them. Use Reports Centre to export the same scope. A month with a missing source is different from a certified month with zero activity.

| Area | Supported and interpretation | Inputs still required for wider coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workforce and retention | Opening/closing active headcount, joiners, confirmed exits, net movement and monthly trends. Monthly attrition divides confirmed exits by the mean of opening and closing headcount. | Reliable gender and birth-date inputs, validated grade/category rollups and dedicated annual/cohort retention measures. Published exits have dates only. |
| Payroll | Reported monthly gross, annual CTC and supplied payroll components. Annualised gross is a run rate of 12 times a complete month. Comparison needs valid, comparable periods. | Source net pay, comprehensive employee and employer deductions, a governed effective-dated statutory rule set, and validated currency conversion. Treat consolidated money reports as INR-only. |
| Attendance and leave | Mean of numeric recorded attendance percentages in scope, including reported zero. Missing measurements are excluded. Non-presence is the complement of the rate and can include approved leave. Leave balance and FYTD utilisation depend on complete and reconciled sources. | Shift-level records, late and early events, approved overtime and an absence classification before publishing absenteeism. |
| Recruitment | Approved and open positions, offers, acceptances, joining and date-based time measures when dates are supplied. | Candidate-level events for a complete funnel, separately attributable recruitment cost and fully mapped target and location dimensions. |
| Performance and increments | Reported scores and approved effective-dated incremental CTC impact. Monthly run rate divides annual CTC impact by 12. | Promotion events, grade comparisons and governed incentive budgets where absent from source. |
| Projects and manpower | Monthly allocation shares, multiple assignments, and cost by payroll gross or allocation gross multiplied by the allocation share. | Work orders and effective allocation intervals within a month. |
| Executive and budget | Current workforce, payroll, attendance, movements and trends, plus comparable budget actuals where dimensions and coverage agree. | Scheduling and delivery require a separately approved service, recipient authorization, expiry, and delivery audit. |

The reporting year starts in April by default and can be configured. Date selection is month plus as-of date. Arbitrary ranges and global grade, status, project and work-order filters are outside the current shared filter model. Employment categories use the roster field. Supplier manpower has its own dataset. See the [analyst guide](hr-analyst-guide.md) for templates and the [release checklist](deployment-readiness.md) for environment setup.

CSV is tabular, Excel is a workbook of allowed reports, and PDF and print use the selected report. Keep the filter context with exported files. Reconcile gross and attendance to the source controls before distribution. If a live export requests refresh, review the refreshed report and export again. If access changes while generating a file, reload the workspace. For missing planning rows, check the reporting period, source selection, scope, and Published state.
